Health Ministry warns against dengue home remedies as cases top 80,000 
By Nidarshani Wickramasinghe 

The Ministry of Health has strongly advised the public not to use any home remedies for dengue other than medicines prescribed 
by a qualified Western medical practitioner or an Ayurvedic physician. The ministry said that under no circumstances should people 
prepare or consume medicines based on the various messages circulating on social media. Dr Saminda Ariyarathna, Consultant Physician 
in Internal Medicine at the National Ayurveda Teaching Hospital, Borella, highlighted that the use of home remedies, particularly 
papaya leaf juice, has caused some dengue patients to become critically ill. He said that, as a result, some patients have suffered 
serious complications. Meanwhile, a specialised dengue treatment clinic will commence at the National Ayurveda Teaching Hospital, 
Borella, from Friday, July 31. Its director, Dr Parakrama Hemachandra invited patients who wish to receive treatment under the Ayurvedic 
system of medicine to visit the hospital for treatment. The number of dengue related deaths reported in Sri Lanka had risen to 61 by 
noon yesterday (25). Health authorities allege that continued public negligence towards dengue prevention has contributed to the 
worsening situation. According to the National Dengue Control Unit (NDCU), the number of dengue cases reported in the country so 
far this year has exceeded 80,910. Of these, 25,530 cases had been reported by July 24. Nearly 47,000 dengue cases were recorded 
during the months of June and July combined. So far this year, 17,135 cases have been reported from the Gampaha District, 16,140 
cases from the Colombo District, and 5,574 cases from the Matara District. The total number of dengue cases reported from the 
Western Province is 42,835. The National Dengue Control Unit reported that 12,260 cases have been recorded in the Southern Province, 
while 6,924 cases have been reported from the Central Province. Meanwhile, entomologists have confirmed that the population of 
Aedes aegypti, the primary mosquito species responsible for transmitting dengue, has declined, while the population of Aedes albopictus 
has shown an increase. Island wide dengue control programmes continue at the national level.

මේ අතර, ඩෙංගු රෝගය සම්ප්රේෂණය කිරීම සඳහා ප්රධාන මදුරු විශේෂය වන ඒඩීස් ඒජිප්ටයි මදුරු ගහනය අඩු වී ඇති බවත්, ඒඩීස් ඇල්බොපික්ටස් මදුරු 
ගහනය වැඩි වී ඇති බවත් කෘමි විද්යාඥයින් තහවුරු කර ඇත.

දිවයින පුරා ජාතික මට්ටමින් ඩෙංගු මර්දන වැඩසටහන් ක්රියාත්මක වෙමින් පවතී.
